WORDS TO FIT IN THE GRID

TOEICGRID

Fit-in puzzle: place every logical connector in the grid, like in the magazines.

Click a place in the grid with the mouse (click again on a crossing to switch direction), then type the word on the keyboard. A correct word from the list is placed; otherwise it is refused.
Letters already given by crossing words are filled in for you.
BACKSPACE: erase a letter  |  TAB or arrows: next place  |  ESC: menu
F1: hint (-50)  |  F2: check (-30 per wrong word)  |  F3: undo. Click a placed word to take it back.
Words are grouped by number of letters (spaces do not count: AS SOON AS = 8 letters). Each word is used once.

Scores are kept on this device.
