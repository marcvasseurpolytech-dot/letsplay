WORDS TO FIT IN THE GRID

TOEICGRID

Big fit-in puzzles like in the magazines: five grids of 150 words each (logical connectors and TOEIC vocabulary, no word is repeated from one grid to the next).

Hover a line to see its number of letters, or the translation of a word already placed (in the grid or in the list). Click a place in the grid with the mouse (click again on a crossing to switch direction), then type the word on the keyboard. A word from the list is placed if it fits; otherwise it is refused.
One word is already placed to start the grid. When a word is found, its French translation glows in a banner and blinks in the list. Letters already given by crossing words are filled in for you. The list is sorted by number of letters; placed words are crossed out.
BACKSPACE: erase a letter  |  TAB or arrows: next place  |  ESC: menu
F1: hint (-50)  |  F3: undo. Click a placed word to take it back. ZOOM buttons resize the grid. Your game is saved automatically on this device.
Spaces do not count: AS SOON AS = 8 letters. Each word is used once.

Scores are kept on this device.
