TOEICGRID

Fit-in puzzle: place every logical connector in the grid, like in the magazines.

UP / DOWN: choose a word in the list
LEFT / RIGHT: choose where it goes
ENTER or SPACE: place it  |  Z: undo  |  H: hint  |  C: check  |  M: sound  |  ESC: menu
Mouse or touch: click a word, then click its place in the grid. Click a placed word to take it back.
Words are grouped by number of letters (spaces do not count: AS SOON AS = 8 letters). Each word is used once, and every crossing must match.

Scores are kept on this device.
